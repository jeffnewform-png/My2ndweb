English Culture & Traditions

Delve into the lifestyle, culinary traditions, and classic customs of England

A Legacy of Lifestyle

English culture is a unique blend of centuries-old customs and modern trends. Famous for its polite demeanor, witty dry humor, and deep-seated traditions, life in England revolves around community, history, and the appreciation of the finer, simpler things.

From social gatherings at local pubs that have stood for centuries, to the iconic sights of vintage double-decker buses weaving through modern metropolitan streets, English lifestyle is defined by the coexistence of its majestic past and pioneering present.

Castles & Legends

Did You Know?

England has over 1,500 historic castles! Scattered across the rolling hills and rugged shores, these structures range from majestic, fully-inhabited royal fortress castles like Windsor Castle—the oldest and largest occupied castle in the world—to picturesque medieval ruins like Bodiam, Kenilworth, and Tintagel, echoing centuries of battles, folklore, and royal intrigue.
